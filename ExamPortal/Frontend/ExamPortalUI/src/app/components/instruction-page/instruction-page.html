Examination Instructions

Before you begin, please review the following guidelines and requirements.

General Instructions

  • This examination consists of 50 multiple-choice questions.
  • Each question has only one correct answer.
  • Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.
  • You can navigate between questions using the navigation panel.
  • You may flag questions for review and return to them later.

Time Management

  • Total time allowed: 90 minutes
  • A timer will be displayed at the top of the exam screen.
  • The exam will automatically submit when time expires.
  • You may submit your exam early if you finish before the time limit.

Technical Requirements

  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection throughout the exam.
  • Do not refresh or close your browser during the exam.
  • Use a desktop or laptop computer for the best experience.
  • Clear your browser cache if you experience any technical issues.

Important Notes

  • Once you start the exam, you cannot pause or restart it.
  • Any attempt to switch tabs or windows may be flagged as suspicious activity.
  • Make sure you are in a quiet environment free from distractions.
  • A passing score of 70% or higher is required to obtain certification.
